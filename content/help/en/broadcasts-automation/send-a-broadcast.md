Broadcasts let you send one message to many contacts.

## Send
1. Open **Broadcasts** and create a new one.
2. Pick an approved template and fill in its variables.
3. Choose your audience: a tag, a segment or a list of contacts.
4. Send now or schedule it.

## Rules to know
- WhatsApp only allows business-started messages through approved templates.
- Customers who opted out are skipped automatically.
- Your plan sets how many broadcast recipients you can reach each month.

After sending, the broadcast page shows delivered, read and failed counts.
