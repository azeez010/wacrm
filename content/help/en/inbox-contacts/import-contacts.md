Your **Contacts** page is the single list of everyone you talk to.

## Import
1. Prepare a CSV file with a name and a phone number in international format.
2. Choose import on the Contacts page and upload the file.
3. Review the preview, then confirm.

## Organise
- Add **tags** to group people, for example by product or city.
- Use custom fields for details specific to your business.
- Save a **segment** to reuse a filter, such as everyone with a given tag.

Phone fields use a country picker, so numbers are stored in a consistent format.
