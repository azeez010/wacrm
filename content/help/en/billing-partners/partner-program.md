Everyone with an OgaDesk account is automatically a partner.

## How it works
1. Open **Partner program** in the sidebar to find your personal link.
2. Share it with businesses that sell or support customers on WhatsApp.
3. Watch clicks, sign-ups and customers appear on your stats.

## What you earn
Partners earn a percentage of eligible payments for a set number of months. The current rate and the earnings calculator are on the public partners page.

## Good to know
- Your first referral code wins: someone can only be referred once.
- You cannot refer yourself.
- Payouts open together with paid plans; tracking starts now.
