OgaDesk uses the official WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta, so your number stays yours.

## Before you start
- A Meta business account with a WhatsApp Business number.
- Admin access to that Meta account.

## Connect
1. Go to **Settings → WhatsApp**.
2. Choose to connect a number and follow the Meta sign-in window.
3. Pick the business account and number to link, then confirm.

Once connected, new customer messages appear in your **Inbox** and you can reply from there.

## Good to know
- Your plan decides how many numbers you can connect.
- Marketing messages to customers use approved templates, as required by WhatsApp.
