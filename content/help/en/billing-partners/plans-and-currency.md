Each plan sets limits such as WhatsApp numbers, seats, contacts and monthly messages. See them on the pricing section of the home page or under **Settings → Billing**.

## Currency
Prices are set in naira. To make them easier to read, we show an approximate price in your own currency using a daily exchange rate. The amount you are actually charged is shown at checkout. Use the currency picker in the page header to switch.

## Upgrading
When you reach a limit we tell you, and you can upgrade from the same screen.
