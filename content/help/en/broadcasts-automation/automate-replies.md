OgaDesk offers three ways to automate.

- **Automations** run an action when something happens, such as tagging a new contact.
- **Flows** guide a customer through a conversation step by step.
- **AI agents** answer common questions from your knowledge library and hand over to a person when needed.

Start with one small automation, watch how it behaves in the inbox, then build on it.
