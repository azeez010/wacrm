---
The **Inbox** shows every WhatsApp conversation, newest first.

## Reply faster
- Use **quick replies** for answers you send often.
- Add internal notes so teammates see context without the customer seeing it.

## Stay organised
- Assign a conversation to a teammate so everyone knows who owns it.
- Close conversations when they are resolved; they reopen if the customer writes again.

## Never miss a message
Turn on the new-message sound and browser notifications under **Settings**, and the unread count shows in your browser tab.
