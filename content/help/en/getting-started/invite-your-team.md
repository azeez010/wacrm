Work together in one shared inbox by inviting teammates.

## Invite
1. Open **Settings → Members**.
2. Enter a teammate's email and choose a role.
3. They receive an invitation link and join your account in one click.

## Roles
- **Owner** and **Admin** manage settings, billing and members.
- **Agent** handles conversations and contacts.
- **Viewer** can look but not change anything.

The number of seats depends on your plan.
