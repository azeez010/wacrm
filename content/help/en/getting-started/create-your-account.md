Creating an OgaDesk account takes about a minute and needs no card.

## Sign up
1. Open the sign-up page and enter your name, work email and a password.
2. Confirm your email using the link we send you.
3. Answer the short welcome questions. If you came through a partner's link, we skip the "how did you hear about us" step.

## Marketing emails
You can switch product news and offers on or off at any time under **Settings → Your profile**. Account and security emails are always sent.

## Next step
Connect your WhatsApp number so conversations can start flowing into your inbox.
